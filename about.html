About itrust Pediatrics

Child & Adolescent Psychiatric Care, With Families At The Center

Who we are. itrust Pediatrics is a psychiatric practice built specifically for children and teens. Our practitioners focus on the mental and behavioral health side of growing up, so your child is in genuinely expert hands.

Our mission. To give children and young adults expert psychiatric care their families can count on. We take big feelings seriously, explain every step in plain language, then stay with you for as long as you need us.

Ages we serve. We care for children and teens ages 5 to 17. When your child turns 18, we'll help transition their care to our adult practice at iTrust Wellness.

What We Do, And What We Don't

We're a psychiatric practice, not a therapy clinic. Our care centers on psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, and medication management, not talk therapy or counseling. When your child would also benefit from therapy, we'll help you connect with a trusted provider for it.

  • Team approach. One practitioner-led team who knows your child, never a revolving door of strangers.
  • Family-centered. Parents are partners in every decision, with clear plans you can use at home.
  • Part of iTrust. We bring the trusted care of iTrust Wellness to children and teens.
  • Paced to your child. Thorough, unhurried, and adjusted as they grow.
