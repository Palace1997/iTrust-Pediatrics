About iTrust Pediatrics

Child & Adolescent Psychiatric Care, With Families At The Center

Who we are

iTrust Pediatrics is a psychiatric practice built specifically for children and adolescents. Our practitioners focus on the mental and behavioral health side of growing up, so your child is in expert hands.

Hover to read

Our mission

To deliver expert child and adolescent psychiatric care that combines accurate diagnosis, evidence-based medication management, and close collaboration with families and other providers to support each child's long-term well-being.

Hover to read

Ages we serve

We care for children and adolescents ages 5 to 17. When your child turns 18, we'll help transition their care to our adult practice at iTrust Wellness.

Hover to read

Our Collaborative Care Model

We provide psychiatric care, and we collaborate for the rest. iTrust Pediatrics offers psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, and evidence-based medication management for children and adolescents. When a fuller approach would help, such as talk therapy or additional testing, we work alongside trusted therapy practices and other professionals, and we help your family navigate services that come from another provider, so your child's care stays coordinated, even when it comes from more than one place.

  • Team approach. One practitioner-led team who knows your child, never a revolving door of strangers.
  • Family-centered. Parents are partners in every decision, with clear plans you can use at home.
  • Part of iTrust. We bring the trusted care of iTrust Wellness to children and adolescents.
  • Paced to your child. Thorough, unhurried, and adjusted as they grow.
