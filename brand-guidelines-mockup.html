Brand Colors

The greens are the heart of our palette: natural, grounded, and full of growth. The coral, teal, and gold hearts add warmth and hope, and the soft creams keep everything feeling gentle.

Lean on the greens as your foundation, and use the heart colors sparingly. Coral is our go-to whenever something should feel like a call to action.

Accessibility & Contrast

Caring for children’s wellbeing means caring about everyone who reads our words, including people with low vision. Good contrast is part of being warm and welcoming.

Use these tested pairings for text. When in doubt, dark ink on cream or white on forest never lets you down.

Target: WCAG AA — at least 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large or bold text.
