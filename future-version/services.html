Some kids have brilliant, busy minds that just work differently. We look past the "behavior problem" label to understand how your child focuses, learns, and thrives, then build supports that play to their strengths.

Signs we watch for

  • Hard time focusing, finishing tasks, or sitting still
  • Big emotions and impulsive moments
  • Struggles with homework, routines, or organization
  • Notes home from teachers about attention

How we help

  • A thorough assessment with input from home and school
  • Behavioral strategies, focus supports, and medication management when appropriate
  • Parent coaching and skill-building support
  • School-support guidance so your child is set up to succeed

It's normal for kids to worry about a test or a thunderstorm. Anxiety is different, it's worry that gets so loud it crowds out school, sleep, friendships, and play. We take those big feelings seriously, with no judgment and no rush.

Signs we watch for

  • Stomachaches or headaches with no clear cause
  • Trouble sleeping, or clinging at drop-off
  • Avoiding school, friends, or once-loved activities
  • Constant "what if" worries that won't switch off

How we help

  • A calm, unhurried assessment of what's really going on
  • Medication management when appropriate, plus proven coping strategies
  • Practical coping tools, mindfulness, breathing, and grounding
  • A family plan, so parents know how to support, not pressure

When sadness lingers for weeks and a child stops being themselves, it deserves real attention, not a "they'll grow out of it." We help families spot it early and build a path back to feeling like themselves again.

Signs we watch for

  • Lasting sadness, irritability, or numbness
  • Pulling away from friends and family
  • Changes in sleep, appetite, or energy
  • Losing interest in things they used to love

How we help

  • A compassionate evaluation and, where needed, safety planning
  • A psychiatric treatment plan tailored to your child, including medication when it helps
  • Practical routines for mood, rest, and daily structure
  • Steady follow-up, we stay with you, and refer if more is needed

Big behaviors, meltdowns, defiance, anger that comes out of nowhere, are usually a child telling us something they can't yet put into words. We help families understand what's underneath and respond in a way that actually calms things down.

Signs we watch for

  • Frequent meltdowns or outbursts beyond their age
  • Defiance, arguing, or trouble with limits
  • Anger or aggression that worries you
  • Conflict that's straining the whole household

How we help

  • Get to the "why" behind the behavior, not just the surface
  • Emotional-regulation strategies and behavioral supports
  • Parent coaching with calm, consistent strategies
  • Family guidance to ease conflict and rebuild connection

Mood that swings, lingering irritability, or ups and downs beyond the usual can point to a mood disorder. We assess carefully, then help your child find steadier ground.

Signs we watch for

  • Big mood swings or ups and downs
  • Irritability or anger that lingers
  • Periods of unusually high or very low energy
  • Trouble that comes and goes in cycles

How we help

  • A thorough psychiatric evaluation and diagnosis
  • Medication management when it's the right fit
  • A clear plan with close monitoring over time
  • Coordination with your family and school

Medication management is a core part of what we do. When it's the right fit, our prescribers offer careful, conservative medication support, closely monitored over time.

What it involves

  • A thorough evaluation before anything is prescribed
  • Conservative, evidence-based prescribing
  • Close monitoring and regular check-ins
  • Coordination with your child's care team and school

Our approach

  • Conservative by default, medication only when it truly helps
  • Clear talk about benefits and trade-offs
  • You and your child are part of every decision
  • Adjusted carefully over time, never rushed

Caring for a child means caring for the whole family. Through psychoeducation, care planning, and guidance from our practitioners, we help parents feel confident supporting their child at home.

What it includes

  • Psychoeducation about your child's diagnosis and needs
  • A clear care plan you help shape
  • Practical guidance from our practitioners for home
  • Support for siblings and the whole family

How it helps

  • Less guesswork and overwhelm at home
  • Calmer, more consistent routines
  • Everyone pulling in the same direction
  • Confidence you're not navigating it alone

One team for your child's mental health. Psychiatric care, medication management, and family guidance all work together here, so your child feels truly understood, never just another appointment.

Our approach

How It Works, From Hello To Ongoing Support

Starting care shouldn't feel complicated. Here's exactly what to expect, step by step.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Book online or give us a call and tell us a little about your child. We'll match them with the right provider.

  2. 2

    Intake & paperwork

    We send simple intake forms and parent paperwork to complete ahead of time, plus any consent or school-release forms, so the first visit is all about your child.

  3. 3

    First session

    Meet your provider, share your story, and start building a plan together, at a pace that feels right.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support & follow‑up

    Regular sessions, check-ins, and steady follow-up, with the whole family supported as your child grows.

Every child deserves care, hope, and to be truly seen. Whole-child pediatrics for every season of growing up.
