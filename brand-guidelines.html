ITRUST PEDIATRICS BRAND GUIDELINES

Brand Essence

Our essence is the heart of who we are. It’s the feeling a family should carry with them after being in our care.

Care & Hope
BRAND ESSENCE
Vision

Our vision is the future we’re working toward, and it keeps everything we do pointed the same way.

We picture a world where every child grows up feeling understood, supported, and hopeful, and where no parent has to face their child’s mental health feeling lost, alone, or helpless.

Mission

This is what we actually do, day to day, to make that vision real.

We give children, teens, and their families compassionate, expert psychiatric care for their mental and behavioral health. That means careful evaluation, diagnosis, and medication management, meeting each child where they are, at a pace that feels right.

Voice & Tone

We know children’s and teens’ behavioral and mental health, but we’re parents and people first. We don’t talk down, we don’t over-promise, and we skip the clinical jargon.

We talk to worried families the way a trusted friend who happens to be an expert would: calm, hopeful, and clear.

BRAND VOICE & TONE
Brand Voice Guidelines

Our voice is warm, reassuring, and down-to-earth. The tone can shift with the moment, but it always stays hopeful, plain-spoken, and kind.

Brand Values

Our values guide the decisions we make, but they only mean something if we actually live them, every single day.

BRAND VALUES
It starts with trust.

Trust isn’t just part of our name. It’s our promise, and we earn it through integrity, reliability, and compassion.

ITRUST PEDIATRICS BRAND GUIDELINES

What We Do

This one’s a firm rule. itrust Pediatrics is a psychiatric practice for children and teens ages 5 to 19, not a therapy clinic. Everything we say and show needs to stay inside that lane.

WHAT WE DO · SCOPE

IN SCOPE

  • Psychiatric evaluation & diagnosis
  • Careful medication management
  • ADHD/ADD, anxiety, depression & mood
  • Behavioral concerns
  • Family-centered treatment (psychoeducation, care planning)

OUT OF SCOPE / NEVER IMPLY

  • Talk therapy, counseling, or play therapy
  • Physical medicine, illnesses, or vaccines
  • Well-child checkups or sick visits
  • Stethoscopes or exam-room imagery
  • Any general-pediatrician / primary-care claim

Logo Usage

The logo should appear in full color when possible. On single-color applications, use the reversed or one-color variations.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the height of the tree icon. Never crowd it. Minimum on-screen width ~120px so the wordmark and hearts stay legible.

Incorrect Logo Usage

DO NOT:

1. Rotate the logo   2. Replace the wordmark font
3. Use a non-brand color   4. Add elements in the clear space
5. Rearrange the order   6. Resize individual parts
7. Outline the wordmark   8. Warp, stretch, or skew
9. Recolor the tree or hearts   10. Place illegibly over images

Photography

WARM, AUTHENTIC & HOPEFUL

Our photos should feel real and reassuring: honest moments among children, teens, families, and our care team. Go for warm light, soft tones, and genuine, positive emotion.

When a child appears, aim for ages 10 to 12, and show real, diverse families. Steer clear of clinical or physical-medicine cues, and always use fresh, properly licensed images.

Iconography & UI

PRACTICAL & APPROACHABLE

Keep icons and interface elements simple, high-contrast, and rounded, echoing the soft shapes of our logo. Stick to brand colors, rounded corners, and pill shapes, and use icons sparingly so they help instead of clutter.

ICONOGRAPHY & UI
Buttons & Icons

Principles

Round the corners everywhere (cards around 14 to 18px; buttons and tags are full pills). Use simple line icons with a ~1.8px stroke and rounded caps, in brand colors. Add gentle, green-tinted shadows for depth, and keep them soft, never harsh.
