Our commitment

itrust Pediatrics is committed to making this website usable and welcoming for everyone, including people with disabilities. We know families often reach us while stressed, on older devices, or on slower connections, and we want the site to work well for them.

Conformance target

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines help make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of abilities. [Confirm your target level once reviewed/audited.]

Accessibility features on this site

  • An on-page accessibility toolbar with options to enlarge text, switch to a more readable font, increase contrast, highlight links, add spacing, pause motion, enlarge the cursor, and hide images.
  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus outlines for interactive elements.
  • Descriptive text for meaningful images and labels for form fields and buttons.
  • Support for reduced motion: animations are minimized when your device requests it.
  • A responsive layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops.
