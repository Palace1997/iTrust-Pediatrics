Medication Management

Medication is never our first or only answer. But when it's genuinely the right fit, careful, collaborative management can be part of a bigger plan. Here's how we approach it.

Our approach

We lead with therapy and holistic support. Medication is only ever considered carefully, alongside therapy, and only when it's truly the right fit, with you part of every decision.

When it might be considered

  • Symptoms are significantly affecting your child's daily life
  • Therapy and support alone haven't been enough yet
  • There's a clear, treatable condition it may help
  • Your family is informed and comfortable exploring it

What to expect

  • A thorough evaluation first, never a rushed prescription
  • A clear conversation about benefits, options, and your questions
  • A careful start with close follow-up
  • Regular check-ins to monitor and adjust
  • The freedom to pause or revisit the plan anytime

How itrust helps

  • Medication considered only as part of a whole-child plan
  • Clear, jargon-free explanations at every step
  • Steady monitoring and follow-up
  • Coordination with therapy and, with consent, your child's other providers

When to reach out

If you're wondering whether medication might help your child, we're glad to talk it through, no pressure. Any decision is made together, with a qualified clinician.

This page is general information, not medical advice or a recommendation for any specific treatment. Medication decisions are always made with a qualified, licensed clinician. If your child is in crisis or danger, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.
