iTrust Pediatrics ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit this website or submit a request through it.

1. Scope

This policy applies to information collected through this website. It does not govern the protected health information (PHI) we handle as a healthcare provider. That information is described in our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices. If any conflict exists, the HIPAA Notice controls for PHI.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you use our appointment or contact form, we may collect the parent or guardian name, email address, phone number, your child's first name and age, whether your child is a new or returning patient, and the details you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we (and our service providers) may automatically collect limited technical information such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and referring pages, through cookies and similar technologies. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services on this website.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule or coordinate care;
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website;
  • To communicate with you about your request;
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

4. Cookies & analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your accessibility preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who host the site or process form submissions on our behalf, under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
  • Legal and safety recipients, when required by law or to protect the safety of a patient or the public.

6. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not include sensitive medical details in website form messages.

7. Children's privacy

This website is intended to be used by parents and guardians, not by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through this site. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Your choices & rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you submitted through this site by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under the laws of South Carolina.

9. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites (for example, our adult practice or a mapping service). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and we will update the "Last updated" date above.
