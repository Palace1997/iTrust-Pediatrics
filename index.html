About itrust Pediatrics

Thoughtful Mental Health Care For Children, Teens, And The Families Who Support Them

itrust Pediatrics is a behavioral and mental health practice built specifically for children and teens. Our team, therapists, counselors, and behavioral specialists, brings deep, focused experience to the emotional side of growing up, so your child is in genuinely expert hands.

That specialization means care that's deeper, not rushed. We work through therapy and holistic, practical approaches, and we partner closely with families from little-kid worries to the big teen conversations. No being passed from stranger to stranger, just steady support from people who remember your last session.

Day to day, that looks like individual therapy, family sessions, play-based work for younger children, teen counseling, and behavioral assessments, each matched to what your child actually needs. Whether it's a few sessions to get through a hard stretch or steady support over the years, we're here for as long as your family needs us.

Therapy and holistic care, working together

We help children and teens work through anxiety, low mood, behavior, focus, and the hard things life throws at them, using proven psychotherapeutic approaches alongside gentle, holistic tools for everyday life. Every plan is tailored to your child and adjusted as they grow and change.

  • Big feelings taken seriously, never brushed off.
  • Individual, family, and play-based therapy.
  • A calm, judgment-free space to open up.
  • Holistic skills, mindfulness, coping, and routines.
  • Care paced to your child's comfort, never rushed.
  • Support for the whole family, not just the child.

Some kids have brilliant, busy minds that just work differently. We look past the "behavior problem" label to understand how your child focuses, learns, and thrives, then build supports that play to their strengths.

Signs we watch for

  • Hard time focusing, finishing tasks, or sitting still
  • Big emotions and impulsive moments
  • Struggles with homework, routines, or organization
  • Notes home from teachers about attention

How we help

  • A thorough assessment with input from home and school
  • Behavior therapy, focus strategies, and steady routines
  • Parent coaching and skill-building support
  • School-support guidance so your child is set up to succeed
Learn more about ADHD

It's normal for kids to worry about a test or a thunderstorm. Anxiety is different, it's worry that gets so loud it crowds out school, sleep, friendships, and play. We take those big feelings seriously, with no judgment and no rush.

Signs we watch for

  • Stomachaches or headaches with no clear cause
  • Trouble sleeping, or clinging at drop-off
  • Avoiding school, friends, or once-loved activities
  • Constant "what if" worries that won't switch off

How we help

  • A calm, unhurried assessment of what's really going on
  • Talk therapy and proven coping skills (like CBT) for worry
  • Holistic tools, mindfulness, breathing, and grounding
  • A family plan, so parents know how to support, not pressure
Learn more about anxiety

When sadness lingers for weeks and a child stops being themselves, it deserves real attention, not a "they'll grow out of it." We help families spot it early and build a path back to feeling like themselves again.

Signs we watch for

  • Lasting sadness, irritability, or numbness
  • Pulling away from friends and family
  • Changes in sleep, appetite, or energy
  • Losing interest in things they used to love

How we help

  • A compassionate evaluation and, where needed, safety planning
  • Individual and family therapy tailored to your child
  • Holistic routines for mood, rest, and daily structure
  • Steady follow-up, we stay with you, and refer if more is needed
Learn more about depression

Big behaviors, meltdowns, defiance, anger that comes out of nowhere, are usually a child telling us something they can't yet put into words. We help families understand what's underneath and respond in a way that actually calms things down.

Signs we watch for

  • Frequent meltdowns or outbursts beyond their age
  • Defiance, arguing, or trouble with limits
  • Anger or aggression that worries you
  • Conflict that's straining the whole household

How we help

  • Get to the "why" behind the behavior, not just the surface
  • Emotional-regulation and coping skills for your child
  • Parent coaching with calm, consistent strategies
  • Family sessions to ease conflict and rebuild connection
Learn more about behavioral concerns

School pressure, focus struggles, or dreading the school day can wear a child down. We help kids feel steadier, more capable, and more at ease with school.

Signs we watch for

  • Dreading school, or refusing to go
  • Falling grades or unfinished work
  • Stress or stomachaches on school mornings
  • Trouble with focus, homework, or organization

How we help

  • Counseling for school stress and confidence
  • Practical focus, study, and organization strategies
  • Guidance on school accommodations (like IEP/504) when needed
  • Collaboration with teachers and counselors
Learn more about school challenges

Friendship drama, exclusion, or bullying can shake a child's confidence and sense of safety. We help kids navigate it and feel like themselves again.

Signs we watch for

  • Pulling back from friends or activities
  • Sudden mood or behavior changes
  • Reluctance to go to school
  • Low self-worth or blaming themselves

How we help

  • A safe space to talk it all through
  • Social skills and healthy assertiveness
  • Rebuilding confidence and self-worth
  • Coordination with the school when it helps
Learn more about bullying support

Caring for a child means caring for the whole family. We help parents and siblings understand what's happening and feel confident supporting their child at home.

What it includes

  • Parent education on your child's needs
  • Practical, doable strategies for home
  • Support for siblings and the whole family
  • A clear, shared plan everyone understands

How it helps

  • Less guesswork and overwhelm at home
  • Calmer, more consistent routines
  • Everyone pulling in the same direction
  • Confidence you're not navigating it alone

When it's the right fit, our prescribers offer careful, conservative medication support, always alongside therapy, never instead of it.

What it involves

  • A thorough evaluation before anything is prescribed
  • Conservative, evidence-based prescribing
  • Close monitoring and regular check-ins
  • Coordination with your child's therapist

Our approach

  • Therapy first; medication only when it truly helps
  • Clear talk about benefits and trade-offs
  • You and your child are part of every decision
  • Adjusted carefully over time, never rushed

Our approach

How It Works, From Hello To Ongoing Support

Starting care shouldn't feel complicated. Here's exactly what to expect, step by step.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Book online or give us a call and tell us a little about your child. We'll match them with the right therapist.

  2. 2

    Intake & paperwork

    We send simple intake forms and parent paperwork to complete ahead of time, plus any consent or school-release forms, so the first visit is all about your child.

  3. 3

    First session

    Meet your therapist, share your story, and start building a plan together, at a pace that feels right.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support & follow‑up

    Regular sessions, check-ins, and steady follow-up, with the whole family supported as your child grows.
