If this is an emergency

If your child is in immediate danger, call 911

Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room right away. If you're worried about your child's safety but it isn't an immediate emergency, the crisis lines below can help you right now, any time of day or night.

Call 911 Call or text 988
Help right now

24/7 crisis lines

Free, confidential, and available any time, every day. You can reach out with questions too, even if you're not sure it's a crisis.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

For anyone in emotional distress or thinking about suicide, and for worried parents who need guidance.

  • Call or text 988
  • Chat at 988lifeline.org

Crisis Text Line

Text with a trained crisis counselor, for when talking on the phone feels like too much.

  • Text HOME to 741741

The Trevor Project

Crisis support built for LGBTQ+ young people, available around the clock.

  • Call 1-866-488-7386
  • Text START to 678-678

Poison Control

If your child may have taken too much medication or another harmful substance.

  • Call 1-800-222-1222
Know the signs

When it may be a mental health emergency

Trust your instincts. If you're unsure, reach out anyway, the crisis lines above are there for questions too.

  • Talking about wanting to die, disappear, or not be here anymore
  • Threatening or planning to hurt themselves or someone else
  • Access to and interest in means of harm, such as medications or firearms
  • Severe agitation or panic, or being unable to calm down
  • A sudden, drastic change in mood, or deep hopelessness
  • Not seeming safe to be left alone
What you can do

Keeping your child safe in a crisis

  1. Stay with your child. Don't leave them alone until you know they're safe.
  2. Put distance between your child and any means of harm. Lock up or remove medications, firearms, alcohol, and sharp objects.
  3. Call 988, or 911 if there's immediate danger. You can call for guidance even when you're not sure what to do.
  4. Listen without judgment. Take any talk of suicide or self-harm seriously, and let your child know you're there for them.
  5. Go to the nearest emergency room if your child isn't safe.

Once your child is safe

When the immediate crisis has passed, reach out to us. We'll help with next steps, revisit the care plan, and make sure your child has steady support going forward.

Contact iTrust Pediatrics

iTrust Pediatrics is an outpatient practice and is not a 24-hour emergency service. In an emergency, please use 911 or 988 first, then contact us.
